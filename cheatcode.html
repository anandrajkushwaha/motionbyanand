01Overview

The shortcut nobody hands you.

People who move fast early in their careers usually aren't smarter. They just had someone to ask: a cousin at Google, a senior who'd read a hundred resumes, a friend who explained what CTC really means.

Cheatcode is that someone, built as a product. It's for final-year students and people in their first few years of work: placements, the first switch, or wondering whether the job they took was a mistake.

Everything a person needs to get started is free with no time limit and no card. Cheatcode Pro adds the AI agent, mock interviews and an expert resume review for ₹99 a month.

02The problem

Under-advised, not underqualified.

Early-career people in India make big decisions with almost no feedback. Every rejection teaches them nothing, because nobody explains it.

You're not underqualified.
You're under-advised.

The gap between you and the person who got the offer usually isn't talent. It's information. That one line became the brief for the whole product.

400+
Applications sent

Three replies.

Most resumes are filtered by software before a person ever opens them. People apply harder instead of differently, because nobody shows them what the software reads.

₹8 LPA
On the offer letter

₹52,400 in the account.

People say yes to a number they don't understand. Gratuity, variable pay, a "retention bonus" in year two. Somebody should have walked them through it.

4 rounds
Cleared

Rejected. No reason given.

HR won't say why. Friends can't. So people guess, change five things at once, and repeat the same mistake in the next interview.

These are the three stories the home page tells. Each one maps to a tool: the ATS checker, the salary calculator, and AI mock interviews with a written report.

04Insights → principles

What shaped every decision.

Before any screen was designed, I wrote down how the product should behave. These four lines settled most arguments later.

i.

Give before you ask.

A first-jobber won't hand over a phone number to a product they haven't tried. So the resume builder, all 60 templates, the ATS checker and the salary calculator work before sign-up.

In the product: pick a template, type, see the resume build. The account is created only when you want to save it.
ii.

Honest beats impressive.

No ATS publishes its scoring, so any tool claiming to return your "real Workday score" is guessing. Cheatcode measures the checkable half and says so out loud.

In the product: "The honest limitation" is a section on the ATS page, not hidden in a footnote.
iii.

Built for India, not translated.

CTC and in-hand pay, LPA, professional tax by state, government notifications, rupees, UPI Autopay, and sign-in with a phone number.

In the product: one plan at ₹99 a month, with no card asked for at sign-up.
iv.

One place, not another tab.

Advice online is written for a million people and none of them is you. When the tools share your resume and profile, the answers stop being generic.

In the product: the AI agent answers with your resume in front of it, and job filters run on your actual profile.

07The product

What I designed and shipped.

Each feature answers one of the stories from the problem section, and each one is live today.

Resume builder

Your resume is ready in ten minutes.

Pick one of 60 ATS-friendly templates, type into a form, and watch the resume build beside it. Switch templates any time without losing a word. Download a clean PDF with no watermark.

  • No sign-up until you want to save
  • Photo upload with crop, zoom and move
  • A line-by-line editor for the final polish
cheatcodeapp.com/build-resume
ATS checker

See what the software sees.

Drop in the resume you're already sending. It's read inside the browser and never uploaded. You get a score out of 100, broken into five parts, and the exact lines costing you points, fixes first.

  • Checks two-column layouts that break parsers
  • Action verbs, numbers, dates and skills
  • Says plainly what no tool can know
Salary calculator

Know what the offer is really worth.

Turn the CTC on an offer letter into the number that reaches your bank account each month. Employer PF, gratuity, professional tax by state, and the old and new tax regimes, line by line.

  • Quick presets from 4 to 18 LPA
  • Every deduction shown, not summarised
  • Written for Indian payslips

08Key design decisions

The calls that mattered.

Each decision traded something away. These are the ones I'd make again.

Challenge

A sign-up wall before the first resume line loses most visitors.

Decision

A guest builder. Pick a template and start typing. The save step uses the same sign-in card as the rest of the app, prefilled from the resume.

Why

People sign up once they've already got something worth keeping, so the account feels like saving their work, not paying a toll.

Challenge

Competitors promise an "exact ATS score". It's a guess dressed as data.

Decision

Score only what can be checked, show each part of the score, and explain the limitation right on the page.

Why

For a product built on "someone who'll tell you the truth", trust is the feature. One honest page is worth more than a flattering number.

Challenge

Students won't enter a card, and email isn't how most of them sign in.

Decision

Google or phone-number sign-in, and a single ₹99 Pro plan on UPI Autopay. No lock-in, no cancellation fee.

Why

One price is easy to explain. UPI is how this audience already pays. The price list fits in one sentence.

Challenge

A new product has no audience and no ad budget.

Decision

Lead with free tools, guides and government-job pages that people already search for, and link them into the product.

Why

Search traffic compounds. Every useful page is a door into the builder, and the tools prove the product before anyone pays.

13Learnings

What I'd tell myself on day one.

Write the principles first.

"Give before you ask" and "honest beats impressive" settled dozens of later decisions in seconds, from the sign-up flow to the copy on the ATS page.

Copy is interface.

The product's voice, plain, specific and a little warm, did as much work as any layout. "₹52,400 in your account" explains the salary tool better than a feature list.

Measure the expensive parts.

AI features feel free while you design them. Tracking cost per feature, per person, from day one kept the ₹99 price honest.

What's next: mentor sessions

One-on-one time with people five to ten years ahead. Mentors are being onboarded first, because a thin mentor pool is worse than none.
